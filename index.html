About

I'm a B.S. Mathematical Sciences candidate (Class of 2028) at the United States Military Academy. My research sits where math meets security. I use topological data analysis to find poisoned training data in network intrusion detection systems, and I study how adversaries could turn everyday technology against U.S. forces.

Outside of research, I compete with the West Point Cyber Team and take photos when I travel.
